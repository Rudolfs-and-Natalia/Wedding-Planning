Leave the driving to us. A shuttle will collect everyone from a meeting point in Lisbon, drop you at the church, then carry on to Solar de Pancas. Around 2 a.m. it brings you back to Lisbon, so there's no car to rent and no designated driver to recruit.

Alenquer is charming but small, with only a handful of places to stay. If you'd like to wake up among the vineyards, rent a house or apartment there, and book early. Prefer city comforts? Stay in Lisbon, just a short drive away, and the shuttle will get you to the party and back.
